Software & File Notes

Software compatibility & expectations.

Transparent compatibility notes so you know exactly how the PPTX decks and documents behave across tools before buying.

Recommended

Microsoft PowerPoint (2016 / 365)

Both 25-slide themes (Paper Studio & Night Studio) were natively built for Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 and Microsoft 365. Full charts, layouts, master styles, and speaker notes work best here.

Canva (PPTX Import)

Canva supports uploading and importing PPTX files, though charts and certain PowerPoint formatting or typography details may not transfer identically and may need minor manual adjustments.

Google Slides & Apple Keynote

Both Google Slides and Keynote can import PPTX files cleanly for editing, though subtle spacing, font fallback, or chart formatting differences can occur during conversion.

Are the slides and worksheets fully editable? +
Yes. All 50 PowerPoint slides (.pptx) let you edit text, duplicate or reorder layouts, swap images, and adjust colors. All planning documents and the Quick-Start Guide include editable DOCX files alongside ready-to-print PDF versions.
Can I use Canva with this toolkit? +
Canva supports importing PPTX files so you can open and customize slides inside Canva. Keep in mind that editable PowerPoint charts and some specific layout formatting may not transfer identically after import and may require minor tweaking.
Do I need Microsoft PowerPoint? +
Microsoft PowerPoint 2016 or Microsoft 365 is recommended for the cleanest typography, charts, and speaker notes experience. You can also import the PPTX files into Google Slides, Keynote, or Canva (with potential minor formatting differences), and all PDF files open in any PDF viewer without extra software.
Can I use the kit for competitions, hackathons, or science fairs? +
Yes. The Project Kit is designed specifically for student projects, hackathons, science fairs, competitions, and pitch showcases — including dedicated Pitch Builder and Judge Preparation worksheets to prepare for judging and Q&A.
Is this a finished presentation or a customizable toolkit? +
It is a complete, customizable toolkit — not a rigid finished deck on a single topic. You receive structured slide layouts, speaker-note guidance, and worksheets so you can adapt the entire presentation quickly to your own project, research, or prototype.
